# what you get

fast
About 170 MB/s of output through a real shell, at 120 fps, drawn with Metal in six draw calls a frame.
tabs and splits
Split any pane right or down, move between panes and swap them, zoom one to fill the tab, drag tabs to reorder.
session restore
Quit and reopen to get your tabs, splits, folders and recent scrollback back.
themes
Ten built in, or your own file; one for light mode and one for dark, switched as macOS switches.
live settings
One plain-text file, applied the moment you save it.
modern output
24-bit colour, curly, dotted and dashed underlines, mouse reporting, wide characters and emoji.
search and links
Find with every match highlighted; Cmd-click a URL or a path to open it.
careful
Programs can't read your clipboard, and pasted text can't smuggle in control characters.
a Mac app
Translucent, blurred backgrounds, full screen, dropped files typed as paths, Secure Keyboard Entry.

# install

  1. Download Shellter.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Shellter onto Applications.
  3. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't signed by Apple yet: click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Shellter.app
